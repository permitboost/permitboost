When do you need a HERS test?

In California, replacing or installing any part of an HVAC system — ducts, condenser, furnace, or air handler — triggers a HERS verification before the permit can be finaled. A certified rater has to test the work independently and register the results.

Our technician performs the required tests on site, usually in 30 minutes to 2 hours depending on the system. Not every test applies to every job; some are exempt based on the scope and existing conditions, and we'll tell you which ones apply before we come out.
