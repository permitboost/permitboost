Common questions

How quickly can an inspection be scheduled?

Availability varies by location, project scope, and route. Advance scheduling is recommended, but contact us with the job details and we will provide the earliest available appointment.

How long does the test take?

Usually 30 minutes to 2 hours on site, depending on the system and how many tests apply. We stay until it's done properly.

What happens if the system fails?

You get the readings and we tell you exactly what's out of range. Once your contractor corrects it, we come back and retest so the job can be finaled.

Do you handle the registry documentation?

Yes. We complete the applicable rater documentation and registry steps required for the field verification work we perform.

Can you help with permits too?

Yes. Permit Boost provides permit-pulling and permit-processing assistance for contractors and construction professionals. Send us the project details so we can confirm the scope we can handle.

What does it cost?

It depends on the scope, the number of systems, and your location. Send the form below with a short description of the job and you'll get a real number back, not a range.
